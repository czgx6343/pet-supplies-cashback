## The fine print that decides whether you actually get paid

Cash back offers come with exclusions, and for pet supplies they matter more than you'd think. Chewy's terms are the most documented, and they exclude several categories pet owners buy constantly:

- **Autoship orders after the first one.** Only your initial subscription order earns cash back; the recurring deliveries don't. Since autoship is Chewy's main selling point, this quietly removes cash back from most people's biggest recurring expense.
- **Pharmacy prescriptions.** Vet-prescribed medications earn nothing.
- **Flea, tick and heartworm products.** Yes, really — the category many pet owners reorder every month is excluded at Chewy. This is exactly why the pharmacy stores matter: 1-800-PetMeds at 10% covers the health-related purchases Chewy's terms skip.
- **Gift cards.** Buying a Chewy gift card earns no cash back.

Payment method matters too. TopCashback's Petco page warns that Safari can't reliably track cash back for that store and recommends Chrome instead. If your cash back never shows up, the usual suspects are an ad blocker, another cash back extension running simultaneously, or clearing cookies mid-checkout. When tracking fails, you can file a "Missing Cash Back" claim, but be prepared to wait — the retailer has to confirm the purchase, and that can take months.

None of this is a reason to skip cash back. It's a reason to know which of your regular purchases are covered before you count on the money.
